The main coastal road through Santa Teresa is paved, but the side roads, hill routes, and many inland tracks are not. Those unpaved sections turn dusty and corrugated in the dry season and can develop mud and standing water in the rainy season. Comparing the best ATV rentals Santa Teresa has to offer helps you find practical transport for those changing conditions.

This roundup considers pricing, automatic and 4x4 options, vehicle delivery, operating support, and suitability for local roads. Rent ATV Santa Teresa is the top overall pick for licensed visitors who want a fully automatic 4x4 delivered to their accommodation, but the other providers suit different vehicles, budgets, and tour plans.

## How we chose the best ATV rentals in Santa Teresa

We compared five direct providers using their published information. No hands-on vehicle test of the other providers was performed.

The review considered starting rates, vehicle choice, Santa Teresa-area coverage, road suitability, delivery, safety equipment, and stated support. Concrete details such as fully automatic transmission, 4x4, helmets, liability insurance, roadside help, taxes, and payment differences counted for more than ratings.

Tripadvisor was left out because it's a review platform rather than a rental provider. Facebook recommendations were treated as anecdotal, and taxis, shuttles, walking, and rental cars were left out because they're alternatives to an ATV rather than ATV companies (the [ATV vs. taxi comparison](/blog/santa-teresa-atv-vs-taxi-which-is-better-for-getting-around) covers that choice).

## Santa Teresa ATV rental FAQs

### How much does it cost to rent an ATV for the day?

At Rent ATV Santa Teresa, daily rates run from $65 for rentals under three days down to $50 for long stays. As listed on their websites in September 2026, ATValerios' Honda 420 starts at $70 per day, and Buggy Blue starts at $75 cash or $85 by card plus taxes. Confirm current rates and which vehicle a rate applies to before booking.

### What are some of the best things to do in Santa Teresa, Costa Rica?

Popular options include surf breaks, restaurants, and the beaches around Playa Carmen, Playa Hermosa, Santa Teresa, and Mal País. Use legal roads, plan longer trips during daylight, and ask for current route advice. Riding on the beach itself isn't allowed.

### Where is the best place to ride an ATV?

The best route is a legal local road suited to the weather and your experience. Ask the provider for route guidance, especially after rain, when mud, ruts, river levels, and water at low points can change conditions quickly.

### Why are ATV rentals so expensive?

ATVs need frequent servicing because local roads can be dusty, corrugated, muddy, and rough. Rates also reflect vehicle costs, tires, insurance, delivery, seasonal demand, roadside assistance, and mechanical support, so compare complete multi-day quotes rather than one advertised daily figure.
