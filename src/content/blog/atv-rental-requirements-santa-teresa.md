The most common snag on rental day is a licence that only exists as a photo on someone's phone, usually while the actual card sits in the hotel safe. So before you book anything, here are the ATV rental requirements in Santa Teresa that actually matter, not the boilerplate nobody reads.

## What you need before you ride

The licence has to be physical. A photo on your phone, a scan in an email, or a description of the number does not count. Bring the actual card. A standard car licence from your home country is enough. You do not need a motorcycle endorsement to ride a quad here.

Closed shoes are the other thing worth sorting out. Flip-flops and sandals do nothing to protect your feet if the quad tips at low speed on a loose corner, and they slip off the footrests on rough ground. Sneakers or old trainers are plenty, and a cheap pair in town works if you only packed sandals.

## The roads decide more than the paperwork does

Santa Teresa's main road through to Mal País and inland to Cóbano is paved, which surprises people who picture this whole area as one long dirt track. What is unpaved is everything else that matters for a day out: the hill roads behind town, the route north toward Manzanillo, and the stretch out toward Montezuma. In the dry season those sections are dusty and corrugated. In the green season they soften, and the low points hold water and mud.

None of that is a reason to skip a quad. It is the reason you want one, especially once you see how spread out [getting around Santa Teresa](/blog/getting-around-santa-teresa) actually is. A regular rental car struggles on the same roads a quad handles without much thought. But it does mean the requirement that matters most is not written on any form: know roughly which roads you are riding before you leave, and slow down the moment the surface changes.

## Picking the right quad for the roads you will ride

Rentals here mostly come down to three models, and which one you need depends more on where you are going than how experienced you are. The Kymco 300cc is fully automatic and the easiest to start on, good for the coast road and short hops between Playa Carmen and Santa Teresa. The Honda 420cc has more power and shifts with your foot, which some riders prefer once they are comfortable. The Yamaha 450cc is the one I would pick for the hill roads, [the ride up to Manzanillo](/blog/manzanillo-beach-atv-santa-teresa), and anything in the green season, because the extra power and clearance matter most exactly where the road gets worst.

Rates start at $70 a day for the Kymco, with the Honda and Yamaha a little more, and 10 percent off any of them once you are renting for eight days or longer. Delivery to your hotel in Santa Teresa, Playa Carmen, or Mal País is included, with a full tank, whichever model you pick.

## Where you can't ride, no matter what

Driving on the beach is against the law in Costa Rica, not a rental-company rule. Ride the road down to the sand, park, and walk the rest of the way. No rental agreement changes that, and the fine is yours if you get caught. My guide to [exploring Santa Teresa beaches by ATV safely](/blog/exploring-santa-teresa-beaches-by-atv-safely) covers exactly where the road ends and the sand begins, beach by beach.

## Conclusion

The real requirements to rent an ATV in Santa Teresa are shorter than the paperwork makes them look: a physical licence, closed shoes, a rough plan for which roads you are riding, and staying off the sand. Get those right and the rest is just picking a quad and a delivery address. Send your dates and where you are staying on WhatsApp, and the rest gets sorted before the quad shows up at your door.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a licence to rent an ATV in Santa Teresa?

Yes. You need a valid physical driver's licence from your home country. A standard car licence is enough. Bring the physical card, not a photo of it.

### Will a US or Canadian driver's licence work?

Yes. A standard car licence from the US, Canada, or most other countries is accepted. Just make sure you bring the actual card, not a picture of it on your phone.

### Can I ride an ATV on the beach in Santa Teresa?

No. Driving on the beach is against the law in Costa Rica. Ride to the beach on the road, then park and walk onto the sand.

### What should I wear to ride?

Closed shoes if you can. Flip-flops and sandals are not safe on a quad. Bring sneakers or pick up a cheap pair in town before you ride.
